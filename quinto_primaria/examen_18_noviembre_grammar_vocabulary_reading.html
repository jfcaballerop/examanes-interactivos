📖 Reading Text: "A Visit to the National Gallery"

Last Saturday, Leo visited the Art Gallery in Madrid with his family. They saw many famous paintings and sculptures. Leo liked a picture called 'The Starry Night'. In the foreground, there is a tall, dark tree. In the background, you can see bright yellow stars and a crescent moon shining over a small village. Leo thought the colors were much brighter and more creative than in traditional portraits. His sister Clara preferred a sculpture of a marble lion. She said it was more impressive than the paintings, even though it was very expensive to make. However, Leo found the modern abstract statues a bit weird and silly. Overall, it was a fantastic day of art discovery!
